    The frame is small.
    The contract isn't.

    A fast first byte is only the beginning. Reliable clients also decide what to ignore, when to stop, and how to close a turn.

    Cancellation has two owners

    Server-side clear and local playback cancellation solve different problems. The client must stop its own queue and reject stale output.

    Context ID documentation ↗

    Warnings and fatal errors diverge

    Branch on stable codes. A warning continues synthesis; a fatal error ends its context without a final frame.

    A simulation is not a benchmark

    These millisecond values are fixture choices. Measure first audio in your real deployment. Pause tags are a Gen2 synthesis feature, not a Falcon streaming control.
