# Visual direction

Mode: operate/read. A warm paper workstation with an ink playback monitor and restrained cobalt controls. An instrument that shows cause/effect, not a marketing dashboard.

First viewport: compact wordmark/offline status, two-line thesis, safe/broken switch, scenario selector and playback monitor. The ledger explains decisions. On phones a native select replaces the sidebar.

Signature: interruption cuts the active segment. Late frames appear in the ledger; the wrong-turn portion is red in broken mode. All motion follows user-controlled simulated time.

Palette: paper #f5f3ee, white #fffefa, ink #18251f, muted #5b675f, rule #d8dcd3, cobalt #214ce2, safe #17694e, warning #8b570d, danger #ae372d. Native sans body, Georgia thesis, ui-monospace data. Spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32/48; corners 6–14px. No remote fonts or assets.

Native keyboard controls, visible focus, semantic headings, text equivalents, reduced-motion styling and no autoplay/audio. Browser layout/accessibility inspection remains a separate release gate.
